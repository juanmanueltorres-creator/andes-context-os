## 3. Important semantic split: “local supplier” has two different layers

### 3.1 Federal RIGI layer

Argentina's RIGI requires a local-supplier development plan and a minimum share of payments to qualifying **local suppliers**.

The federal definition is national: qualifying suppliers are Argentine persons/entities meeting the RIGI definition. It does **not** mean that the complete minimum percentage must be spent specifically in Jujuy.

The current federal regulation states that goods and/or works from local suppliers can count toward the minimum 20% established by the RIGI framework.

Primary sources:

- Argentina.gob.ar — updated Decree 749/2024, Article 49: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/decreto-749-2024-403230/actualizacion
- Argentina.gob.ar — RIGI overview: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/504415
- Argentina.gob.ar — RIGI supplier FAQ: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/2024/12/preguntas_frecuentes_-_proveedores_rigi.pdf

### 3.2 Jujuy provincial layer

Jujuy independently operates the **Registro Provincial de Proveedores Locales de Productores Mineros**.

The registry is active in 2026 and is designed to classify and register local mining suppliers and support development of the provincial mining value chain.

For legal entities, the current digital form requests, among other items:

- corporate and fiscal domicile;
- CUIT;
- activity/categories;
- contact data;
- tax and corporate documentation;
- employee information by province;
- supporting residence/local-presence documentation.

The registration process is online and the provincial site states that registration has no cost.

Primary sources:

- Jujuy supplier registry: https://proveedores.registrominero.jujuy.gob.ar/
- Legal-entity registration form: https://proveedores.registrominero.jujuy.gob.ar/registro/persona-juridica
- Provincial mining registry landing page: https://registrominero.jujuy.gob.ar/
- Jujuy Official Gazette — Resolution 229-DEyP/2024: https://boletinoficial.jujuy.gob.ar/?p=302403
- Jujuy Official Gazette — Resolution 104-DEyP/2026: https://boletinoficial.jujuy.gob.ar/?p=433360

### Product rule learned

```text
RIGI local supplier != Jujuy local supplier
```

Both may matter commercially, but they represent different legal/market-access concepts and must never be collapsed into one field or claim.

## 4. Verified supplier-entry channels

### Channel A — Jujuy provincial supplier registry

**Status:** VERIFIED CURRENT

The provincial registry is live, public, digital and accepts supplier applications.

What it proves:

- there is a current formal provincial supplier-registration path;
- companies can declare their activities/categories;
- Jujuy has a policy instrument intended to make local mining suppliers visible and classifiable.

What it does **not** prove:

- that registration guarantees access to EXAR purchasing;
- that EXAR uses this registry as its only supplier gate;
- that a registered company has an active opportunity at Cauchari-Olaroz.

### Channel B — Federal mining / RIGI supplier infrastructure

**Status:** VERIFIED CURRENT

Argentina maintains a Federal Mining Suppliers Registry and supplier-support resources for strategic investment chains.

Primary sources:

- Federal Mining Suppliers Registry: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/economia/mineria/inscribite-en-el-registro-federal-de-proveedores-mineros-rfpm
- Suppliers for strategic industry / RIGI: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/economia/industria-comercio-y-pyme/proveedores-de-bienes-y-servicios-para-la-industria

What it proves:

- a supplier can create formal federal visibility;
- supplier location, products and services are part of the federal supplier data model.

What it does not prove:

- that EXAR sources all purchases from these databases;
- that being listed means being invited to Stage 2 procurement.

### Channel C — EXAR direct supplier onboarding

**Status:** NOT CURRENTLY VERIFIED AS A PRIMARY ACTIVE PROCUREMENT PORTAL

Historical evidence shows EXAR had a direct provider registry. A 2017 article states that providers could register through Minera Exar's website.

A 2026 PromPerú supplier guide still references:

`https://www.mineraexar.com.ar/alta-proveedores/`

and states that registered companies may receive notices of procurement processes.

However, in this research pass the current EXAR website exposed a general contact form but did **not** surface a current primary page explicitly labeled as supplier/procurement onboarding.

Sources:

- Historical EXAR supplier-registration reference: https://www.panorama-minero.com/es/news/minera-exar-abre-sus-puertas-a-proveedores

### Boundary

Do not convert the secondary 2026 reference to the old supplier URL into a claim that EXAR's current procurement portal is verified and operational.

That remains a research gap.

- EXAR — Stage 2 RIGI submission: https://www.exar.com.ar/exar-presenta-su-solicitud-de-ampliacion-del-proyecto-cauchari-olaroz-al-rigi/

### Interpretation

```text
expansion != empty supplier market
```

A new supplier may face:

- incumbent vendors;
- qualified supplier pools;
- framework agreements;
- community/local content expectations;
- technical and compliance requirements;
- packages potentially sourced through strategic shareholders.

This makes procurement-channel research more important than raw demand inference.

## 11. Product findings for Andes Context OS

### Finding A — One opportunity can depend on multiple regulatory/market-access layers

The same phrase — `local supplier` — referred to two different constructs:

```text
federal RIGI local supplier
!=
Jujuy provincial local mining supplier
```

Andes must preserve the authority and jurisdiction of each rule.

### Finding B — Negative evidence is commercially valuable

Not finding a current primary EXAR procurement portal is not a failure.

It prevents the system from generating a fake action such as:

> "Register here and you will receive Stage 2 tenders."

A safer result is:

> "A historical/secondary supplier-registration route exists, but the current direct EXAR procurement path remains unverified."

### Finding C — Expansion can close some opportunities while opening others

Ganfeng's role in DLE equipment fabrication means that Stage 2 may **reduce** the addressable opportunity for independent core process-equipment vendors while still increasing demand for site integration, civil works, utilities and services.

Therefore:

```text
project movement
    ↓
does not only create opportunities
    ↓
it also changes opportunity boundaries
```

### Finding D — Actor discovery should include incumbent suppliers

Opportunity research should not only ask:

> Who could sell to this project?

It should also ask:

> Who already sells to this project, in what category, and under what evidence?

Existing supplier networks change the competitive interpretation of an opportunity.

## 12. Round 2 conclusion

The original broad hypothesis:

> "Cauchari-Olaroz is expanding, therefore suppliers have an opportunity."

is too weak.

The evidence-supported version is:

> **Cauchari-Olaroz Stage 2 is moving into early development under RIGI, with additional wells, infrastructure and site preparation already identified. Argentine supplier participation is structurally relevant under RIGI, while Jujuy maintains a separate provincial supplier-development registry. EXAR already operates with a large incumbent supplier ecosystem. The strongest near-term research targets are wellfield/civil/infrastructure packages and the exact procurement/onboarding path; core DLE process equipment appears more constrained by Ganfeng's internal fabrication role.**

That is actionable enough to direct the next research pass without pretending that a tender already exists.
